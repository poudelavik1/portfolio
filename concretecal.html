Assumptions
  • Brick 240 × 115 × 57 mm with 10 mm joints, so each brick takes 250 × 125 × 67 mm of wall.
  • Wet mortar = wall volume − volume of the bricks themselves.
  • Dry mortar = 1.33 × wet mortar, split by the mortar ratio.
  • Cement density 1440 kg/m³, 50 kg bags. Openings are deducted in full.
